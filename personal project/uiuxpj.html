UI/UX Projects

A collection of user-centered design solutions, spanning from high-fidelity mobile prototypes to complex web dashboards.

Core Experience: E-Commerce Mobile App

Project Goal & User Research

The goal was to streamline the mobile shopping experience for a fashion retailer. Through user interviews and survey data, I identified friction points in the checkout process and developed a simplified, one-page checkout flow.

Research Insights: - Users preferred visual cues over text-heavy product descriptions. - High abandonment rates were linked to complex shipping calculations.

Platform Visual: SaaS Analytics Dashboard

Design Process & Tools

I follow a rigorous design thinking methodology, starting from low-fidelity wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes, ensuring consistent brand identity through a dedicated Design System.

  • Design Software: Figma, Adobe Illustrator
  • Methodology: User-Centered Design (UCD), Atomic Design
  • Key Artifacts: User Personas, Wireframes, Interactive Prototypes, Style Guides
