The Design Process

My approach ensures every project begins with a deep understanding of user needs and business goals, leading to a functional and beautiful final product.

  • Discovery & Research: Analyzing target audience, competitors, and defining project goals.
  • Wireframing & Information Architecture: Structuring content and defining the user flow (the backbone of the interface).
  • High-Fidelity Prototyping: Creating the final visual design, animations, and interactive mockups.
  • Usability Testing: Gathering feedback to iterate and refine the design before development begins.
