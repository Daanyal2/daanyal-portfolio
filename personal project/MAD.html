  • Native Coding: Utilizing the best framework (Java/Kotlin) for stability and speed.
  • Testing & Quality Assurance: Rigorous testing on multiple devices to ensure a bug-free experience.
  • Deployment & Support: Launching on Google Play/App Store and providing post-launch support.
